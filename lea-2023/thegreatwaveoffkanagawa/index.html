The Great Wave off Kanagawa

The Great Wave off Kanagawa is a two-player local wave making and fighting game. Made during 2017 Global Game Jam, finished in 48 hours. In this game, two players stand on opposite sides of the screen, fighting against each other by creating great wave as much as possible.

Project Type: Digital Game

Date: 2017

Members: Lea Liu, Song Ye, Emma Wang, Feious Su

My Role: Game Designer, Game Artist
