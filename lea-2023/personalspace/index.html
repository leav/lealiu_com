Personal Space

Personal Space is a top-down view walking simulation game made during 2017 Columbia University Game Jam. In this game, player walks to their destination on a crowded street with the Porcupine Law with other passengers in mind - neither stay too far nor too close to them

Project Type: Digital Game

Date: 2017

Members: Lea Liu, Song Ye, Wangshu Sun

My Role: Game Designer, Game Artist
