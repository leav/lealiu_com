Tian Long Ba Bu

Tian Long Ba Bu was one of the most popular MMORPG last decade in China developed by Changyou.inc, based on a famous Wuxia (Kung Fu) novel: The Semi-Gods and the Semi evils by Jin Yong.It's a 45 perspective 3D game incorporate with a WoW-like system.

The background of the game is set in ancient China, but some of the paid contents provide some exotic items and characters out of the game world. That is the reason my designs on this page include many different themes.

Project Type: MMORPG

Date: 2012-2014

Developer: Changyou.com

My Role: Game Concept Artist, UI Designer

Website:http://tl.changyou.com/
