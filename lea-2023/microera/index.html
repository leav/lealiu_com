MicroEra

Micro Era is a visual prototype for a Sci-fi platform game. The project includes a set of concept art and a mock-up video which depicts a post-apocalyptic world.

In this world, the human was exterminated by unknown a disaster, but there still a few of them survived by an astonishing way: shrink themselves as large as bacteria. They live in a tiny city with microbes, take fungus as their palm trees and yeasts as their livestock.

These pieces show how the world and art style was built.
