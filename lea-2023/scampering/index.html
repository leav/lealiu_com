Scampering Carnival

Project Type: Fan-Art Project

Date: 2012

Member: S.O.G. Game Club

My Role: Organizer, Manager, Promotor, Illustrator, Contributor

Scampering Carnival is a Fan-Art project made during 2011-2012. It is about the interesting stories happened in the S.O.G Game Club, an online group consist of mostly Monster Hunter players. We designed a series of characters based on the club members. Writers put them into stories based on real event, and illustrators turn these stories into mangas. We published these stories and mangas as an art book, and also produced some buttons, stickers, and keychains at the same time. We traveled to various comic cons in Beijing, Shanghai, and Wuhan in China to sell it during the first half of 2012.
