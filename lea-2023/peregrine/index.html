Peregrine

Made as a semester long project in Matt Boch and Bennett Foddy’s Studio II class, Peregrine is a explore-and-survive game where you guide a small tribe across a vast steppe. The player plays as the chieftain leading the tribe to food and shelters. The decisions player made determine the fate of the tribe.ade as a semester long project in Matt Boch and Bennett Foddy’s Studio II class, Peregrine is a exploration based survival game where you guide small tribe across a vast steppe.
