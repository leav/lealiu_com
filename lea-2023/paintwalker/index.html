Paint Walker

Paint Walker is a VR experience created in collaboration with Metropolitan Museum, this project allows player explore the topography of oil paint, which sometimes reminiscent of pigment mountains and valleys.The idea of the project is to lead player understanding an artwork in a brand new perspective and have fun in this exploring process.

Project Type: VR Experience

Date: 2016

Members:Sam Von Ehren, Erenyx Qiu, Lea Liu, Emma Wang

My Role: 3D Artist, UI Designer
