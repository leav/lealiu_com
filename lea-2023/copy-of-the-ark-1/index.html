Saita Fridge

Saita! Fridge

Santa fridge is a mobile casual game inspired by the stay-at-home time during COVID19, our whole life has been shrunk into a small room and household works, for specific, cooking and organizing fridge all the time.

In this game, there are only two things you should do: organizing your new groceries into a fridge, and taking items out of fridge according to a memo listed ahead. The more memo you finished, the higher score you can get, that's all.

Project Type: Casual Game

Date: 2021

Members: Lea Liu, Yalin Zhang, Song Ye, Siyao Xu, Wangshu Sun

My Role: Lead Artist
