Table of Theseus

Table Of Thesues

Table Of Theseus is a casual puzzle game. The stage of the game is set at a birthday party. An uninvited beaver suddenly shows up and begins nibbling at the leg of the cake table. To save the cake from its horrible fate, the player needs to find replacements for the table legs to keep the table balanced, which leads to a series of chain disasters. The cake ends up intact, but the backyard winds up with a total mess. It is a funny and cutesy style mini-game.

Project Type: Puzzle Game

Date: 2021

Members: Lea Liu, Yalin Zhang, Song ye, Teng Ma, Wangshu Sun, Zhiqiang Hao

My Role: Artist
