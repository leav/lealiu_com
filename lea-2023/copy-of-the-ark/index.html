Cats Are Weird

Cats Are Weird! is a puzzle game. It is a game to find cats hidden in the scene. The player receives a mysterious “boss cat” request to find his missing employee cats. The game uses a bizarre artistic style that blends cute cats with Cthulhu elements and incorporates some of the internet popular cat memes into its design.

Project Type: Puzzle Game

Date: 2021

Members: Lea Liu, Yalin Zhang, Teng Ma

My Role: Artist
