BlueComi Online

A personal website provided Japanese ACG (Animes, Comics, and Games)information, which included three parts: a news blog provided journals with images, a fan-art platform which received fans' contributes, and a BBS having 2000 users at its peak.

Making a website was my primary hobby when I was in junior school, and this site could be said one of the earliest Teenager-made websites in China, and since then, I started to learn Photoshop, HTML, CSS and web design.
